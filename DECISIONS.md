# Product and implementation decisions

1. Build in the empty D:\carbuyers workspace. There was no existing code or Git repository to preserve.
2. Keep the requested Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind and Vercel target. Sites skill guidance informs workflow and QA; its Cloudflare/Vinext hosting default is superseded by the explicit Next.js/Vercel stack.
3. Launch with eleven tools and six researched guides. Use a handful of distinct financing scenarios, well below the latest 20–30 maximum. Do not generate pages to reach a count.
4. Financial models are educational. Defaults are illustrative inputs, never live offers. APR approximates a nominal rate only when fees do not cause them to differ. Users can enter the contract interest rate.
5. Valuations use a visible editable depreciation model, not licensed market data. No make/model-specific price claims or appraisals.
6. Sales tax is calculated from a user-entered verified combined rate, taxable fees, and explicitly entered allowed trade-in credit. State selection links to official agencies; it does not silently choose current rules or rates.
7. No invented author, qualifications, expert review, operator identity, or contact inbox. An Organization byline identifies site-produced content; operational contact is environment-configured. Document launch blockers honestly.
8. No fabricated update dates. Publication date is omitted until editorial publication is approved; displayed methodology version is a software version rather than a research date.
9. Ads remain disabled without a real publisher ID, slot IDs and certified CMP integration. Built-in preferences cover optional analytics and do not claim to replace a certified advertising CMP. No placeholder ads.txt or affiliate links ship.
10. Public content uses static rendering. Calculator state hydrates from the URL in client islands; canonical URLs never include queries. Sensitive personal details are not requested. Shared numerical inputs are visible in URLs.
11. No SearchAction without working site search, no Person schema without a real person, no claim of rich-result eligibility. Structured data describes visible content only.
12. Follow the user's automotive visual references: white and pale green surfaces, deep navy type, forest-green controls, spacious calculator cards, and original generated editorial photography. Local WebP images use responsive Next.js image delivery; icons and diagrams remain SVG/CSS. Images illustrate topics and never represent real listings, customers, or market evidence.
13. Extend the same design across tool and guide hubs, category collections, long-form articles, all calculator pages, curated scenarios, informational pages, and the 404 page. Preserve formulas, source references, explicit estimate limitations, working privacy controls, and accessible navigation while restyling.
