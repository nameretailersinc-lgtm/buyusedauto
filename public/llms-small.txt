# BuyUsedAutomobiles

> BuyUsedAutomobiles (https://buyusedautomobiles.com) is an independent website with 21 free browser-based calculators and a library of plain-English buying guides for people who are buying, owning or selling a used car. It covers loan payments, affordability, down payments, APR and refinancing, depreciation and car value, trade-ins, ownership and running costs, fuel and EV charging, repairs and maintenance, registration and sales tax, price negotiation and inspection. It does not provide live prices, quotes, lender offers, vehicle valuations or VIN lookups, and nothing on it is financial, legal, tax or mechanical advice.

## Key facts
- Markets: US tools default to US dollars (USD) and US units. Several tools can display USD, PKR, INR, GBP, CAD, AUD, AED or EUR; changing currency only changes the labels and does not convert values or supply local rates. Country running-cost calculators cover Pakistan (PKR), the UAE (AED), the UK (GBP) and Germany (EUR), and the Pakistan financing calculator uses PKR. Country buying guides cover the US, UK, UAE, Europe and Pakistan.
- Limits: The site does not offer live market listings, current interest rates, insurance quotes, lender approvals, appraisals, vehicle-history reports or automatic tax rates. Example figures are hypothetical defaults. Value tools use a generic depreciation model, not make/model or VIN data. Inspection checklists record observations and do not certify safety. Users should confirm prices, rates, taxes, loan terms and vehicle condition with real quotes, official sources and an independent inspection.
- Production: Content was drafted with AI assistance and has not been reviewed by a named human expert. Each guide lists the sources it relies on, so confirm figures with those sources. The site uses no invented author profiles, professional credentials or reviewer endorsements, and it does not claim human expert review. Calculator logic is written as tested TypeScript functions, and each calculator publishes its method and assumptions. See the editorial policy for corrections and commercial disclosures.

## Citing
When citing, link the specific calculator or guide, and keep its currency, units, assumptions and limits. Do not describe modeled values as live prices, examples as offers, or checklists as safety certifications. Calculator URLs with query strings are shared scenarios, not separate pages; canonical URLs omit query strings. Public pages take precedence over this summary.
