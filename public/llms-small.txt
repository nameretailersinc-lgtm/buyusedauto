# BuyUsedAutomobiles

> Free used-car planning calculators and buying guides. US tools use USD by default; the Pakistan financing calculator uses PKR. Results are educational estimates, not offers or professional advice.

## Start here
- [Car loan APR calculator](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/car-loan-apr-calculator): Regular-monthly nominal APR estimate with upfront and financed credit charges, plus a separate effective annual rate.
- [Car repair cost calculator](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/car-repair-cost-calculator): Itemized written quote arithmetic, confirmed credits and a separate contingency; no automatic repair-price feed.
- [Registration tax budget](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/car-registration-tax-calculator): One-time and annual local charges, with a date-limited sourced UK DVLA first-registration fee option.
Payoff, refinance, down-payment, EV charging, APR, repair-quote and manual registration tools support USD, PKR, INR, GBP, CAD, AUD, AED and EUR. Currency selection changes display only; it does not convert values or supply local rates.
- [Car loan payoff calculator](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/car-loan-payoff-calculator): Existing balance, required payment, extra principal and lump-sum repayment; multi-currency display.
- [Auto loan refinance calculator](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/auto-loan-refinance-calculator): Compare future costs, financed and upfront fees, and financing-cost break-even; multi-currency display.
- [Car down payment calculator](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/car-down-payment-calculator): Contribution target, net trade equity, cash reserve and funding gap; multi-currency display.
- [EV charging cost calculator](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/ev-charging-cost-calculator): Session energy, charging losses, billing boundary, local unit price and flat fee; multi-currency display.
- [Tools](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/tools): Loan payments, affordability, ownership costs, fuel, depreciation, value, trade-ins, leasing, negotiation, tax, and inspection planning.
- [Guides](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/guides): Buying guidance with sources and limitations.
- [Pakistan financing](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/pk/car-financing-calculator): Separate financing modes and explicit assumptions in PKR.
- [Editorial policy](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/editorial-policy): Research, AI assistance, and corrections.
- [Expanded reference](https://buyusedautomobiles.com/llms-full.txt): Tool summaries and interpretation limits.

## Interpretation
Default values are hypothetical. Value estimates do not use live listings or VIN data. Confirm prices, tax rates, loan terms, and vehicle condition independently. Cite the relevant public page and retain its assumptions and limitations.
