## Scenario pages (individual review)

Six scenario pages are in the sitemap. Each has its own written intro, considerations and FAQ tied to a distinct decision: repair reserve at $10k, down payment at $25k, full-budget check at $40k, a 0% promo, a 4-year baseline at 8%, and a 72-month 12% stress test. They are not mass-generated permutations, so retain. Do not add more amount or rate permutations.

## Content architecture decisions

- Retain `/guides/how-to-calculate-percentage-increase`: it exists as a submitted guide, is off-topic to the core audience, and is weaker than the finance guides. Keep it linked only from relevant guides. Revisit once GSC shows whether it earns impressions.
- No merges or deletions recommended yet; no overlapping canonical conflicts found by the audit.
- No hreflang: country guides are not verified translations.
