## Scoring without invented metrics

`global-keyword-research.csv` contains Appendix A seeds and the requested Pakistan bank variants. Every volume, KD and CPC field is UNVERIFIED. Row confidence is `unverified`: a seed or a source title supports terminology, not exact query demand. `date_checked` is the intake/context-review date, not proof of a metric lookup. New target URLs in either CSV are unpublished candidates; no country or article launch is approved by the map.

Requested keyword score: relevance 30%, opportunity 20%, feasibility 20%, user value 15%, monetization 10%, effort 5%, all normalized to 0–100. Opportunity cannot be estimated responsibly without demand/KD/GSC; omit that component and divide the remaining weights by 80. Effective weights: relevance .375, feasibility .25, user value .1875, monetization .125, effort .0625. Missing KD is never zero. Other values are explicitly **estimated editorial judgments as of 2026-10-15**, not external observations. Higher effort score means less work; monetization means suitability for a useful commercial decision, not measured CPC or earnings.

These exact profile vectors generate the CSV scores. Profile assignment follows existing intent fit: reliable/best-used and average-payment seeds require evidence; new/used, salary eligibility, PCP/balloon and novated-lease queries have capability gaps; non-US routes need localization. A score never overrides a source or launch gate.

Cluster by country and decision intent. Primary is a provisional representative seed, not a proven head term. Bank variants are secondary in the same Pakistan financing cluster; they do not justify bank-specific routes. No volumes are summed; overlap is not unique traffic. `keyword-page-map.csv` mirrors each research row's owner. New/used comparison stays with the existing ownership tool. Early payoff and loan-term queries stay with the existing loan engine. Broad salary queries are partial fits to affordability, not eligibility promises. Biweekly/fortnightly and local tax queries need actual implementation before their candidate pages can satisfy intent. Real SERP-overlap analysis and GSC can split/merge these provisional groups later.

USA + Pakistan first, then India, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE. Preserve root US URLs. All later countries remain research-only. A country route ships only with an approved market, functional localized calculator, two genuinely localized guides, native terminology review where needed, primary-source rules, and owner capacity for updates. Self canonicals and reciprocal hreflang/x-default only between truly equivalent pages; per-market sitemap segmentation follows actual route inventory.

Request GSC query/page/country/device exports with reporting period and a previous comparable period. Use actual existing impressions/clicks to revise primary queries and intent owners before creating routes. Request licensed keyword exports per market database and language; until then no metric-based opportunity, KD ranking or traffic/revenue estimate is defensible. Bank presets need an official applicable document URL, period/effective date, checked date and complete fee/eligibility interpretation. Reliability articles need dated accessible model-level evidence plus matching local price evidence; otherwise omit rankings and publish no placeholder.
