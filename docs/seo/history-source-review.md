# Car-history guide source review

Checked: 2026-10-16. Scope: published source text supporting `/guides/how-to-check-a-cars-history`, not a check of an individual VIN, title or recall result. No independent professional review is claimed.

| Primary source | Support used in the guide | Limits retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [DOJ NMVTIS consumer guidance](https://vehiclehistory.bja.ojp.gov/nmvtis_consumers) | Title brands, reported mileage and salvage/total-loss records; approved consumer providers; state record access. | State terminology and reporting differ. Missing records do not establish clean condition. No historical coverage percentage or report price adopted. |
| [NICB VINCheck](https://www.nicb.org/vincheck) | Free search using participating insurers; theft and salvage/flood scope. | Not a complete history or law-enforcement search. No insurer participation statistic adopted. |
| [NHTSA recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) | VIN-specific open recall lookup and its exclusions. | Completed repairs, some newly announced recalls, non-safety campaigns and international vehicles are excluded; older-recall coverage is limited. No claim that a zero-result VIN is safe. |
| [NHTSA odometer fraud](https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/odometer-fraud) | Compare displayed mileage with title, maintenance and inspection evidence. | Wear alone does not prove rollback; no universal mileage cutoff or incidence statistic adopted. |
